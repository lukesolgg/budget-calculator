Orcl.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 June 2026

Orcl. is an anonymous personal-finance planning tool. This policy explains what we store, why, and the control you have over it. Plain English, no surprises.

Who we are

Orcl. is an independent, non-commercial personal project. For any privacy or data-protection question, contact lukeknightdev@gmail.com.

Accounts are anonymous

You create an account with a username and a PIN only. We do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, or postal address. We cannot personally identify you from your Orcl. account.

What we store

  • The figures you enter — income, bills, debts, savings goals and similar planning inputs you type into Orcl.
  • If (and only if) you link a bank — read-only account details, balances and recent transactions retrieved through Open Banking, so Orcl. can show your spending. We never receive or store your bank login credentials.

Your data is stored in our cloud database (Supabase) so you can sign back in from any device. It is associated with your anonymous username, not with you personally.

Bank linking (Open Banking)

Bank connections are provided through Enable Banking, a regulated Open Banking provider. The connection is read-only: Orcl. can see balances and transactions but can never move money. You authenticate directly with your bank — your bank login never passes through Orcl. Consent lasts around 90 days, after which you choose whether to reconnect. You can disconnect a bank at any time from Settings, which deletes the imported data.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties for marketing.
  • We do not show ads or run advertising trackers.
  • We do not build a profile of you to sell on.

Your control

  • Export — download a full copy of your data as JSON at any time (Settings → Your data).
  • Delete — wipe all your data and your account at any time (Settings → Delete my data). This is permanent.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Bank API secrets are held server-side and never exposed to your browser. No system is perfectly secure, but Orcl. is built to hold as little identifying information as possible.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change too. Material changes will be noted in the app.

Orcl. is a pre-alpha preview and provides general guidance, not financial advice.
